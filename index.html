💪 Gym Protocol: Tuesday

  • Squats

    3 sets of 8-12 reps

    Use a barbell, standing with feet shoulder-width apart and then lower your body by bending knees.

  • Leg Press

    3 sets of 8-12 reps

    Sit down on the leg press machine, place your feet on the platform, push the weight away with your feet and lower it back down.

  • Lateral Raises (Shoulders)

    3 sets of 10-15 reps

    Hold a dumbbell in each hand at your sides. Lift the dumbbells outwards, keeping your elbows straight, and lower them back down without moving your upper arms.

  • Dumbbell Shoulder Press

    3 sets of 8-12 reps

    Hold a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height. Press the dumbbells straight up and lower them back down.

  • Upright Row (Shoulders)

    3 sets of 8-12 reps

    Hold a barbell or a cable handle in front of your thighs. Lift the bar upwards towards your chest and lower it back down.
